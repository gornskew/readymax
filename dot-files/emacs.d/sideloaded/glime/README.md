Glime, vendored
===============

glime.el and glime.lisp here are copies of emacs/glime.el and
emacs/glime.lisp in Gendl (https://gitlab.common-lisp.net/gendl/gendl).
Gendl's are the originals: change them there and copy both files here
again; do not edit these.  etc/slime-config.el loads glime.el, and
glime.el sends glime.lisp to a Gendl image that lacks it or has an
older one.
